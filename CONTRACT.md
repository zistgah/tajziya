# CONTRACT: tajziya

This overlay adds to the master contract at zistgah/governance and never relaxes it. Where the two
differ, the master wins. Each clause names the check that enforces it (`ops/verify.sh`, V01 to V17)
and the failure or source that earned it.

**T1. A node is keyed by stage, script and corpus, never by a name alone.**
Enforced by V06. Earned by poster I §8 ("a diachronic model, not Mandarin") and §5 (do not delete
the stage).

**T2. An unbuilt layer refuses by name and names the package that builds it.**
It never returns a plausible analysis. Enforced by V07 (TJZ-C1, TJZ-C2) and V05, which proves the
harness catches a port that fabricates. Earned by the spine's D10 and its Unknown primitive.

**T3. Ambiguity is returned whole.**
Every reading the rules admit is returned; none is chosen silently, and output too large to
enumerate is Unknown rather than truncated. Enforced by V07 (TJZ-C3) and V03. Earned by the
upstream parser: a merged vowel can come from several pairs.

**T4. A rule enters with its source.**
No rule text is written from memory, however standard it looks. Until a sutra is sourced, the
junction record says so. Enforced by the SOURCE stage of both cyclers and by V03. Earned by the
upstream CONTRIBUTING.

**T5. Third-party material follows its own terms.**
It is attributed, and what its terms do not let us repost is neither committed nor deposited; only
its hash is. Enforced by V08. Earned by the sutra compilation, whose source states that its texts
are not to be reposted without permission.

**T6. Translation is not recovery.**
Enforced by V03 (test_translation_is_never_recovery). Earned by poster III: translation
equivalence does not establish linguistic redundancy.

**T7. Nothing is eliminated without evidence, and a minimum is never certified while the corpus
universe or the loss function is open.** Even then the certificate is relative to what was declared.
Enforced by V03. Earned by poster III's closing line and the §20 decision rule.

**T8. Script, language and standard are separate axes.**
Families are shared by descent; engines are shared by phenomenon. Enforced by the axis field in
`registry/engines.json`, by V14 (the spine's leak check) and by V03. Earned by the ILM rulings of
30 Aug 2026 and the spine's D9.

**T9. The Python and JavaScript ports of the parser stay behaviour-identical, proven by execution.**
Enforced by V04 and by a mutation test that changes one rule and must be caught. Earned by the
upstream CONTRIBUTING.

**T10. A conflict in the source is recorded and held. The machine does not resolve it.**
Enforced by V06. Earned by poster I §14, whose first row is labelled Ge'ez over the Orkhon
inscriptions.

**T11. Every script here stays inside the folder it runs in.**
Master clause 7, applied: no home directory, no /tmp, no parent path, and no source read from outside the
repository folder. Dependencies are cloned inside it. Enforced by V18, and by the refusals in `data import`
and `tools/vendor_import.py`. Earned by the first seed script for this repository, which read estate.sh
from a path outside its run folder that no repository holds.

Reconstruct faithfully. Critique with evidence, not tone. Maintain contract and context.
