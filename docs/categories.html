The Aṣṭādhyāyī's order is not a topical order. Here you choose the categories and which sutras go in them, with the checkboxes, or by uploading a CSV. Nothing here changes the review page or the data; your arrangement stays in this browser until you download it.

An arrangement CSV has one row per sutra and category: id,category, for example 6.1.77,vowel sandhi. A sutra may be in several categories. A first row of id,category is read as a header.
