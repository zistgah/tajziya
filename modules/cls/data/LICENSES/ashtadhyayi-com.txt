"You are free to use this data in your own projects provided that appropriate credits are
mentioned wherever applicable."

data/ashtadhyayi/sutraani.tsv is derived from it by tools/sutra_import.py: the number, the
Siddhanta Kaumudi number, the type, the defined term, the text and the padaccheda of each of
the 3,983 sutras, re-encoded and otherwise unchanged. Credit for the data is ashtadhyayi.com's.
